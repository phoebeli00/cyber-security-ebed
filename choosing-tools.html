Resources & definitions

Information classification summary

Public: little or no risk if disclosed, e.g. research publications.

Private: moderate risk; the default for data not marked Public or Restricted.

Restricted: significant risk if disclosed; the highest level of controls applies.

Adapted from CMU's Guidelines for Data Classification.

Definitions

Account ownership: who the account belongs to and who manages it: CMU or you personally.

Storage: where the file is kept: university storage, a commercial cloud, a laptop, or a provider's servers.

Access: who can open the file, and who decides that.

Data handling: which agreements and practices apply, such as retention or using inputs to improve models.

Tool selection checklist

1. Classification and project requirement · 2. Account owner · 3. Storage · 4. Access and who manages it · 5. Agreements and data practices
